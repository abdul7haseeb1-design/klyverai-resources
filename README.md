## 📖 Overview

Traditional keyword rankings are giving way to Answer Engines. Large language models (LLMs) and generative search tools synthesize multi-source answers rather than just serving blue links. This repository provides a tactical framework for structuring websites, technical architecture, and content to ensure your brand is cited as a primary source.

---

## 🗂️ What’s Inside the Playbook

### Module 1: The Shift from SEO to AEO
* **Entity vs. Keyword:** Why traditional keyword rankings are shifting to entity-based citation frameworks.
* **The AI Overview Architecture:** Decoding how Google, Perplexity, and ChatGPT aggregate and source web data.
* **The Klyverai Audit Test:** How to check if your domain is being crawled and parsed efficiently by citation bots (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot).

### Module 2: Technical Architecture for AI Crawlers
* **Advanced JSON-LD Schema:** Moving beyond basic article markup into explicit entity-attribute mapping.
* **DOM Parsing Speed & Clean Code:** Why performance-first architectures (Next.js/Jamstack) give you a massive advantage over bloated CMS setups.
* **Robots.txt & Meta Directives:** Managing LLM access to block low-value web scrapers while welcoming high-converting citation crawlers.

### Module 3: Content Structuring for Generative Extraction
* **The "Inverted Pyramid 2.0":** Structuring paragraphs to front-load direct answers in the first 40 words.
* **Data-Dense Formatting:** Utilizing clean markdown tables, explicit statistical callouts, and bulleted frameworks that LLMs love to pull verbatim.
* **Authority Signals (E-E-A-T):** Establishing subject-matter authority networks that language models recognize and trust.
